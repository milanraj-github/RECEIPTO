Privacy Policy for Receipto

Last Updated: November 2, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Welcome to Receipto ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the Receipto mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Receipts & Expense Data: When you scan or upload receipts, we process the images to extract vendor name, transaction date, line items, taxes, currency, and total amounts.
  • User Notes & Categories: Custom categories, tags, and notes you assign to your receipts.

B. Device & Permission-Based Access

  • Camera Access: Used solely to capture physical receipts for scanning. We only access the camera when you deliberately trigger the capture button.
  • Photo & Storage Access: Used only when you choose to import an existing photo or PDF from your gallery.
  • Device Diagnostics: Basic device model and crash logs used strictly to ensure app stability.
🔒 Financial Security Guarantee: Receipto NEVER asks for, collects, or stores your bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, PINs, or online banking passwords.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide core application features:

  • To extract and categorize expense details using AI and OCR.
  • To compute spending analytics, monthly category breakdowns, and trends.
  • To generate downloadable PDF invoices and expense summaries.
  • To securely sync your receipts across your authorized devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We work only with reputable, enterprise-grade cloud providers:

  • Supabase: For secure database storage and authentication.
  • Google Gemini API: For state-of-the-art receipt text parsing. Data sent for parsing is processed securely and is never used to train public models.
  • Google Play Services: For secure app distribution and update delivery.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest with AES encryption. Access to your receipts is strictly restricted to your authenticated user account.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete Receipts: You can delete any receipt directly inside the app at any time.
  • Delete Account: You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at milanraj660@gmail.com. All data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.
