# Privacy Policy for Receipto

**Last Updated:** November 2, 2026

Welcome to **Receipto** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the Receipto mobile application (the "App").

By using Receipto, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
- **Receipts and Expense Data:** When you photograph, scan, or upload receipts, invoices, or bills, we process images and extract information including merchant name, transaction date, line items, taxes, currency, and total amounts.
- **User Notes and Categories:** Custom categories, tags, notes, and payment methods you assign to your receipts.

### B. Device and Permission-Based Information
- **Camera Access:** We request permission to access your device camera solely to photograph physical receipts for scanning. We only capture images when you deliberately trigger the camera within the app.
- **Photos / Storage Access:** We request read access to your device storage/photo gallery only when you choose to import an existing photo or PDF of a receipt.
- **Device Information:** Standard diagnostic metrics such as device model, operating system version, and crash logs to improve app stability and fix technical issues.

> **Note on Financial Credentials:** Receipto **NEVER** asks for, collects, or stores your bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, PINs, or online banking passwords.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve the Receipto service:
- To scan, extract, and categorize your receipts using optical character recognition (OCR) and artificial intelligence.
- To organize your personal expenses and generate financial insights, monthly analytics, and spending summaries.
- To generate exportable PDF invoices and spreadsheet summaries upon your request.
- To securely sync your expense data across your authorized devices.
- To provide customer support and troubleshoot application defects.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Data Processing

To deliver core app functionality, we partner with industry-leading, secure cloud providers:
- **Cloud Database & Authentication (Supabase):** Used for secure account management, encrypted storage of your receipts, and real-time database synchronization.
- **Artificial Intelligence & OCR (Google Gemini API):** Used to read and extract text from receipt images. Image data sent for parsing is processed securely in accordance with strict API privacy terms and is not used to train public foundational models.
- **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution, updates, and core platform diagnostics.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

We employ robust physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect your data:
- All communications between the app and our backend servers are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- Data stored in our databases is encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES encryption.
- Access to your personal receipts is strictly restricted to your authenticated user account.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership of your data at all times:
- **Receipt Deletion:** You can delete any individual receipt or expense record directly from the app at any time, which permanently removes the associated image and data.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** You may request complete deletion of your account and all associated receipts by emailing us at [milanraj660@gmail.com](mailto:milanraj660@gmail.com). Upon receipt of your request, all personal data linked to your account will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.
